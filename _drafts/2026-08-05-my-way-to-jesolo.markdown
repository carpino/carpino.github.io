# Near The End

At the time of writing this post I am 33 and I can say that I have not been very sportive in my life. I my early 20s I practiced some activities like climbing (bouldering) and rowing or had some short runs and weight lifting from time to time. In my early 20s I weighed around 78kg but all changed in 2019 when I was diagnosed with depression.

From that moment on I started to gain weight and I removed all the sportive activities i used to practice. At Jenuary 2026 my weight was 118 kg and at the age of 33 I had high Colesterhol, high blood pressure and I was near to Diabethes other than high values of aminotransferasi ALT and AST.

My focus on these years from 2019 to 2026 was mainly on my mind, at all towards my body.

According to professor Paul Raymond Gilbert, the founder of Compassion Focused Therapy, our mind is characterized by three sets of states

![Three emotions system](/assets/images/3emotions.jpg)

The green system is when we feel calm and reassured, our life is full of the warmth of true connections and we are safe. The blue one is the dopaminergic system, when we feel excited and motivated. The red one is when we feel not safe. When those systems are not balanced they can create a psicopathology.

In these years I was living in the red one, I missed almost completely the emotions and states of the blu system.

Then in February 2026 something changed.

# A New Begining

February 2026 was a new begining.

I started to look at cyclism as a way to explore nature and places, mountain passes, small mountain villages, lakes, valleys...

Cyclism also has a low impact on articulations compared to other cardio sport and is more easy to practice compared to running, at least with small distancing and without climbings. Cyclistic performances can also be measured and so data can be collected are usefull for comparison with ourselves.

I had this idea when i started, to bike over the same places over and over again and try each time to do a personal best. I was seking that thrill of a good performance without having any bad impact on any living being.

And I remembered the calm and relaxation after the sportive performance, indipendently from the performance, due to endogenous opioids, the green system !

And so I got my first road bike on a budget, a Triban RC 500.

![Triban 500](/assets/images/triban500.jpg)

Soon after i realized that at my latitude February is not a good day to start to bike. And soon my first bicycle trainers arrived, a Kickr Core 2.

![Rulli](/assets/images/kickrCore2.jpg)

That was the beginning.
